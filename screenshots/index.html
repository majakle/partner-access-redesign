Partner access redesign — how it works

Annotated screenshots of Version E (the current redesign), plus what changed versus the previous live form and why — based on SUS, interviews, and heuristic findings.

Interactive form: partner access form · Full rationale: IMPROVEMENTS.md

Flow overview Screen by screen What improved & why

Flow overview

Registration is framed as a partner access request, not instant account creation. Existing users are steered to Sign in; new partners move through four short steps with a progress bar.

Start Partner with us — benefits & made for trade
25% Contact person — name, work email, optional phone
50% Company — find by name/VAT or enter manually
75% About your business — optional profile + terms
100% Confirmation — email → review → reply in 3 days

Screen by screen

Gateway

1. Partner with us

Entry page — premium invite tone before any form fields.

Why: Replaces “Create account” with partner application framing (P1). Separates Sign in from the primary CTA (P2). States benefits and audience so the wrong visitors leave early.

Step 1 of 4 · 25%

2. Contact person

Required name & work email; phone optional with country dial code.

Why: Splits the old long form into short steps with progress (P3). Purpose microcopy and required * markers reduce confusion (P5). Phone stays optional so it doesn’t block submission.

Step 2 of 4 · 50%

3. Company — find by name or VAT

Demo: search “Company XY” → confirm card → Use these details.

Why: Cuts manual company + VAT + address typing (P5b). Confirm-before-commit prevents wrong autofill. Manual entry remains as fallback.

Step 2 · after confirm

4. Company details applied

Fields prefilled; postal + house lookup can refine the address.

Why: Users verify and edit rather than retype. Address lookup (PDOK / Photon) matches the Benelux autofill pattern users asked for.

Step 3 of 4 · 75%

5. About your business

Website / social / about are optional; only terms are required to submit.

Why: Mandatory website blocked offline/studio partners (P4). Optional profile still helps review, without gating access. CTA says “Request access,” not “Create account.”

Step 4 of 4 · 100%

6. Confirmation

Warm thank-you plus clear next steps and a 3-business-day review — access is not active yet.

Why: Post-submit silence was a top interview complaint (P6). Full page (not a dismissible popup) avoids accidental loss (P7). Language control stays in the header (P8).

What improved vs the previous form

Previous live experience: hybrid sign-in + “create account,” long single form (often in a popup), mandatory website, heavy manual entry, and unclear review timing. Mean SUS in the study: 63.75 (below ~68).

Problem Previous form Version E Why it matters
P1 Wording “Create account” implies instant access “Partner with us” / application + review before access Matches manual review; protects trust after submit
P2 Sign-in vs register Both compete on one view Primary CTA = request; Sign in in header + secondary link Fewer wrong first actions
P3 Length One long page / heavy scroll 4 steps + progress % Lower perceived effort; clearer status
P4 Website Mandatory Optional (with purpose hint) Doesn’t block studios without a site
P5 Guidance Weak examples / late errors Examples, optional badges, required *, dial code Fewer late surprises
P5b Entry effort Manual company + address Name/VAT find + address lookup Less typing; fewer typos
P6 After submit Vague “we will review” Email → review → 3 business days Trade buyers can plan
P7 Container Popup risk (click outside closes) Full pages only No accidental data loss
P8 Language Incomplete / mixed locales NL / EN / DE in header throughout Signals full-locale intent

Retained from the old form: clear labels, company → address logic, VAT as B2B check, no password at request stage.
