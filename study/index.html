Usability Evaluation of a Business to Business Registration Process

Thank you for participating in this research.

This study evaluates and compares the usability of two versions of a business-to-business registration process. The research is being conducted as part of a master’s thesis at the University of Maribor, Faculty of Economics and Business. The findings will be used to evaluate whether the redesigned registration process improves the user experience compared with the existing version.

Participation should take approximately 15–30 minutes.

What you will be asked to do

You will be asked to complete the same defined registration task using two different versions of a business-to-business registration process. You will be asked to have your screen recorded.

Depending on the assigned order, you will either:

  • complete Version A first and Version B second, or
  • complete Version B first and Version A second.

After completing each version, you will answer ten statements about its usability. After both versions, you will answer several comparative questions about your experience.

Please use only the fictional test information provided. Do not enter any real personal, contact, or company information.

You will also be asked for limited background information, including your professional role, previous experience with business-to-business portals, and the device used during the evaluation.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time before submitting without providing a reason. You may also skip any question that is not marked as required.

Participation involves no anticipated risks beyond those normally associated with using and evaluating an online registration form. Your participation will not affect your relationship with any company, the researcher, or the university.

Data collection and use

The study will collect your questionnaire responses, your evaluations of both registration versions, limited background information, screen recording of the registration tasks, and automatically measured task times.

The collected data will be treated confidentially. Any identifying information will be removed before the results are analysed or presented.

The collected information will be used exclusively for research connected with the master’s thesis. Results will be presented in an aggregated or anonymized form so that individual participants cannot be identified.

The raw research data will be stored securely until 1 October 2026. After that date, the raw data will be permanently deleted. The data will not be shared with unauthorized third parties. The video recording will be deleted right after analysis.

How screen recording works

Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a laptop/desktop (not Safari, not a phone).

For each version you will click one button. Your browser will ask what to share first — choose Window → Google Chrome (or Edge). Then the form opens in a new tab inside that window so only the browser (not your whole desktop) is recorded.

On a Mac, Chrome must be allowed under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording. If sharing never appears, enable Chrome there, quit Chrome completely, and reopen it.
